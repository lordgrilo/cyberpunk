# Grimoire — Hypotheses

## H-001: An unwitnessed lock is unenforced

**Status:** partially confirmed; scope unresolved

> Nobody is watching the lock or door; no one is enforcing its locked status.

**Evidence for:** The Threshold Testimony acted when Mara and the security camera could
see the first attempt. It was silent—and the locked door opened—after Mara left the
corridor and the camera's view was blocked.

In a discriminating second test, the fourth-floor lift camera had an unobstructed view of
Room 404 while no human watched. The Threshold Testimony was again silent and the locked
door opened. A device witness alone is therefore insufficient under the current rule.

**Confounds:** The evidence distinguishes the camera from Mara, but does not yet establish
whether direct human sight, human presence, attention, testimony, or some correlated
property is the actual enforcing condition.

**Next discriminating experiment:** Compare an attentive human witness with a sleeping,
blindfolded, or indirect human witness.

## H-002: Room 404 is an administrative replacement for Room 412

**Status:** strongly supported; consequence unconfirmed

The hotel is preparing to make its records for Room 404 more authoritative than the
physical existence and occupancy of Room 412. The transition is scheduled for 06:10 on
2 August. "Quarantine duplicate room" may mean that Room 412, Nadia, or both will cease
to be accepted by the hotel's reality.

**Evidence for:** The key labeled 404 was originally engraved 412. Nadia's certificate
distinguishes physical lodging 412 from consensus lodging 404. Room 404 is a converted
service space whose telephone mirrors line 412. The work order schedules communications,
optical records, and vacancy reconciliation immediately before 06:10. Nadia's recording
explicitly distinguishes the physical room from its record.

A call placed physically from Room 404 was identified by hotel service as originating
from Room 412. An order placed for "this room" was therefore entered for delivery to 412,
confirming that the mirror is operational before the scheduled reconciliation.

**Possible countermeasure:** Nadia's note asks for someone to see her in Room 412 before
06:10. Direct human testimony may compete with the hotel's institutional account, and
current lock experiments show that human and optical witnesses are not equivalent.

**Unknowns:** "Quarantine" could still be mundane institutional action rather than
ontological deletion. It is not known whether the player counts as a qualifying witness,
whether one witness is enough, or whether witnessing must occur at the effective time.

**Discriminating evidence:** Establish Nadia's presence directly; compare what the hotel
records before and after; determine whether an independent human witness changes any law
signature or administrative state.
